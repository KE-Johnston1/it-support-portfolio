# Case Study: Investigating Legitimate Email in Quarantine

**Category:** Email troubleshooting and security awareness  
**Environment:** Simulated service desk  
**Skills illustrated:** Evidence review, false-positive analysis, cautious remediation and documentation

> This is a simulated learning exercise. It does not demonstrate administration of a live mail security platform.

## 1. Scenario

A user reports that expected internal emails have not arrived. The simulated mail trace indicates that some messages were quarantined by a spam filter.

## 2. Investigation

1. Establish when the issue began and which messages are missing.
2. Review the relevant mail trace window and focus on events from the reported period.
3. Inspect the evidence associated with the quarantined messages.
4. Determine whether the messages appear legitimate or suspicious using the information available.
5. Follow the approved process for releasing confirmed false positives and leave genuine or uncertain suspicious messages quarantined for further review.

A spam score or quarantine status is evidence to investigate; it should not be treated as conclusive proof by itself. Do not release messages solely because a sender claims they are legitimate.

## 3. Resolution and verification

In the simulation, the scenario identifies legitimate internal messages that were incorrectly quarantined. The appropriate simulated response is to release only the messages confirmed to be legitimate, then verify delivery where the environment allows it.

In a real organisation, follow mail-security policy and escalation procedures. If the evidence is inconclusive, seek review from the appropriate security or messaging administrator.

## 4. Documentation

Record the time window reviewed, relevant trace findings, the basis for classifying the messages, the action taken and whether delivery was verified. Avoid including message contents or personal data in a public portfolio.

## 5. Learning points

- Use evidence to investigate missing messages.
- Distinguish a false positive from genuine spam or phishing.
- Apply the least risky corrective action.
- Keep uncertain messages quarantined and escalate when needed.
- Verify delivery and document the outcome.
